Computes the persistent homology of a complex over \(\Z/p\Z\) coefficients, where \(p\) is prime. Implements a parallelized version of the standard persistence algorithm in line with that of Edelsbrunner and Harer, incorporating a just-in-time (JIT) version of the twist optimization. Convenience overload.

Template Parameters
RingLikeA coefficient Ring, like Zp or Q.
Parameters
complexA (pointer to) a Complex with a flat boundary matrix.
filtrationA vector that specifies the order in which to add the cells in the flat boundary matrix. For example, if we are doing 1-dimensional percolation in 2-d, then we switch up the ordering of the 1-d cells in the flat boundary matrix, but leave everything else.
R(Pointer to) a coefficient Ring, like Zp or Q.
resourcesParallel computing environment resources.
Returns
A vector of percolation times.
